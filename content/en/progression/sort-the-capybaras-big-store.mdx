## How Sort the Capybaras Actually Works

At its core, Sort the Capybaras is a matching puzzle. Capybaras arrive on a board in different varieties — colors, hats, accessories, or poses, depending on the version you're playing — and your job is to move them into matching groups so they clear and free up space.

When the board fills with no valid moves left, the run ends. That simple fail state is what gives the game its tension, and it's also why progression matters so much. Every upgrade you unlock makes the board a little more forgiving, and every new capybara type you add to the pool changes how sets get built.

The loop is easy to describe:

- Clear boards and earn coins
- Spend those coins in the shop on capybara types, boosters, and board space
- Use your new unlocks to clear harder boards
- Harder boards pay better, which funds the next round of unlocks

| Loop Stage | What Happens | What It Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Early boards | Simple sets, generous space | Coins and a feel for the mechanics |
| First shop visit | Small purchases, one or two upgrades | Extra slots or a basic booster |
| Mid game | Denser boards, tighter space | New capybara types and stronger boosters |
| Late game | Boards punish wasted moves | Cosmetic goals and completionist unlocks |

Community reports consistently describe the early game as forgiving and the mid game as the point where one careless move can end a run. That's the moment the Sort the Capybaras big store stops being a novelty and becomes part of your actual strategy.

### What to Buy First

If you're staring at the Sort the Capybaras big store with a small pile of coins, resist the urge to buy whatever looks cutest. Player experience suggests a simple order works best:

1. **Anything that adds board space.** More room means more breathing room, and more breathing room means fewer dead ends.
2. **One reliable booster.** A single shuffle or undo in your pocket can save a run that would otherwise wipe out your streak.
3. **A couple of new capybara types.** Variety keeps boards interesting, but it also increases the number of distinct sets you're juggling.
4. **Cosmetics last.** They're fun, and they're worth it eventually — just not before your fundamentals are solid.

### What You Can Safely Skip

- Duplicate boosters you rarely trigger
- Cosmetic bundles when you're still failing boards
- Anything that only pays off if you play for hours in one sitting

## Sorting Strategies That Speed Up Your Progress

Good sorting habits compound. A player who sorts efficiently earns coins faster, which means more trips to the store, which means smoother progression overall.

| Strategy | How to Apply It | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| Group before you place | Scan for existing pairs and triples first | Prevents scattering matching capybaras |
| Keep an overflow lane | Leave one area deliberately loose | Gives you somewhere to park stragglers |
| Work from the edges inward | Clear outer groups before the middle | Preserves central flexibility |
| Watch the incoming queue | Plan around what's arriving next | Reduces surprise dead ends |
| Bank boosters | Save them for genuine walls | Turns certain losses into clears |
| Accept "good enough" | Stop chasing perfect boards | Saves moves and time |

A few habits worth building on top of that:

- **Count before you move.** A two-second scan often reveals a better first move than the obvious one.
- **Don't break a finished set to fix a messy one.** Finished sets are progress; messy ones are just noise.
- **Play shorter, sharper sessions.** Long sessions lead to autopilot, and autopilot leads to wasted moves.
- **Revisit old boards.** Easy clears are the most reliable coin source when you're saving for a big purchase.

## FAQ

**Is the Sort the Capybaras big store worth spending coins on?**
Yes, with one caveat: spend on things that change outcomes. Board capacity and boosters change outcomes. Cosmetics don't. If you're ever unsure, buy the upgrade that prevents a specific failure you keep running into.

**What should I buy first in the store?**
Start with anything that increases your board space, then add a single reliable booster. Those two purchases tend to produce the biggest jump in clear rate for the smallest spend, and they make every later purchase more affordable.

**Do I need to buy everything to finish the game?**
No. Community reports suggest that most progression goals can be reached with a focused set of upgrades. Completionists will eventually want the cosmetic and convenience items, but they're optional for clearing boards.

**Can I progress without visiting the store at all?**
You can make some headway, but progress slows dramatically once boards get denser. The Sort the Capybaras big store exists to keep the difficulty curve fair, and skipping it entirely means playing every hard board with the tools you started with.
