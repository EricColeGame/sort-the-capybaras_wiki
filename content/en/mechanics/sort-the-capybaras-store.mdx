### Why Slots Beat Skins

Sorting puzzles get hard when your tray runs out of room. A single extra slot can be the difference between a clean finish and a board that locks up with three unmatched capybaras staring at you. Cosmetics change how the game looks; slots change whether you win.

## How Sorting Mechanics Shape Your Shopping List

The core loop is simple: tap a capybara, move it to a matching group, and clear the board before you run out of space or moves. Every purchase in the sort the capybaras store either expands your capacity, extends your margin for error, or does neither.

That gives you a clean filter. Before you buy anything, ask which of those three buckets the item falls into. If the answer is "none," it is a cosmetic purchase — fine if you love it, terrible if you are stuck on level 40.

### The Slot Economy

Tray expansions compound. Each new slot makes every future level slightly more forgiving, and that forgiveness stacks across dozens of levels. Power-ups are consumed; slots are permanent. When currency is tight, permanence wins.

### When Power-Ups Are Correct

Power-ups stop being a waste the moment you hit a level that has eaten three of your attempts. Buying a shuffle to break a stubborn board is cheaper than grinding ten easy levels to earn back the same coins. Treat power-ups as insurance, not as a crutch.

## FAQ

**Is the sort the capybaras store worth spending real money on?**

For most players, no. Everything mechanically important can be earned through normal play, and paid bundles mostly save time. Buy only if you want to support the developer or you specifically want a cosmetic set.

**What should I buy first in the sort the capybaras store?**

A permanent extra holding slot. It helps on every level you play afterward, unlike a power-up that disappears after one use.

**Do capybara skins affect gameplay?**

No. Skins and themes are purely visual in every version the community has documented. They are fun, but they will not help you clear a hard board.

**How often does the shop rotate?**

Yes. Free currency sources cover the essentials, though progress will be slower than for players who buy slot upgrades early. Patience is a legitimate strategy here, and honestly, it fits the capybara theme.
